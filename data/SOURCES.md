# Corpus sources

Every file in `data/corpus/` is text from a US federal health agency page. Works of the US government are not copyrightable (17 U.S.C. section 105), but a page can embed third-party material, so each document has a license check. Text was extracted with `scripts/fetch_doc.py` and then cleaned by hand: navigation and boilerplate removed, wording unchanged.
